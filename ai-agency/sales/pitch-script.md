# Pitch scripts

The product sells itself if you get the demo on their phone. Every script below has one
goal: **get permission to run the 2-minute demo.** Never explain AI. Never say "automation."
Say "missed calls" and "booked jobs."

## The walk-in (highest close rate — use this)

Best time: 1–3 PM on a weekday, when the office is quiet. Ask for the owner or office manager.

> "Hey — quick question, I'll be 30 seconds. When you guys are out on jobs and someone
> calls the office, what happens to that call?"

*(They'll say voicemail, or "my wife answers when she can," or "we call back later.")*

> "That's what I figured — it's the same for almost every [HVAC/roofing/plumbing] company
> I talk to. The problem is 8 out of 10 people who hit voicemail just call the next company
> on Google. I fix exactly that one problem. Can I show you on your phone? Takes two minutes
> — call this number and don't let me pick up."

*(Run the demo: they call your demo line / open the demo page, watch the instant text-back,
tap through to a booked job.)*

> "That whole conversation happens automatically, on your existing number, sounding like
> your office. It's $297 a month — less than one service call — and if it doesn't book you
> a job in the first 30 days, the setup fee comes back. Want me to have it running on your
> line by Friday?"

## The phone version

> "Hi, is this [Name]? This is [You] — I'm local, and I'll be quick. I help [trade]
> companies catch the calls they miss when the crew's on a job. Quick question: roughly how
> many calls a week would you guess go to voicemail?"

*(Any answer works.)*

> "So even at [their number], with your average ticket, that's serious money going to
> whoever answers next. I've got a two-minute demo that runs on your phone — no meeting,
> no slides. Can I text it to you right now?"

Then text the demo link. Follow up the same evening: "Did the demo book you? 😄 Want that
running on your real line?"

## Objections — the only four you'll hear

**"How much?"** — "$997 to set it up, $297 a month, month to month. One rescued service
call covers it; one rescued replacement job covers the year. And the 30-day guarantee means
the risk is on me."

**"My customers hate robots."** — "They hate *voicemail*. This texts them back in ten
seconds and answers their actual question. Nobody's ever complained about getting helped
too fast. And it never pretends to be human."

**"I'll think about it."** — "Totally fair. While you think — want me to leave it running
in shadow mode for a week? It won't text anyone; it'll just count the calls you missed and
what they were worth. Then the numbers do the thinking." *(This close is deadly. Almost
nobody says no to free data about their own business.)*

**"What if it says something wrong?"** — "It can only say the things you approve — your
prices, your hours, your service area. We build that sheet together and you sign off before
it sends a single text. Anything off-script, it takes a message and pings your cell."

## Rules

- Demo before price, always. Price without the demo is just a number; price after the demo
  is a discount on money they're already losing.
- Talk to owners, not gatekeepers. If the office manager blocks you, ask: "Who cries when
  the phone rings and nobody grabs it? That's who I need two minutes with."
- Ask for referrals on day 7, not day 90: "Who else do you know that's out on jobs all
  day? Intro me and I'll knock $100 off your next month."
