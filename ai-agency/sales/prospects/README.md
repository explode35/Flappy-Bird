# Prospect list — Triangle-area HVAC

Two files, same format, together covering the whole Triangle:

- **`holly-springs-hvac.csv`** — 14 businesses in Holly Springs and immediately adjacent
  Fuquay-Varina (your home base).
- **`triangle-hvac-expanded.csv`** — 27 more businesses across Fuquay-Varina, Apex, Cary,
  Garner, Wake Forest, Morrisville, Clayton, Knightdale, Raleigh, Durham, and Chapel Hill.

**41 real HVAC businesses total**, verified phone numbers pulled from BBB/Yelp/company
sites. Open either in Sheets/Excel/Numbers, or on your phone via Google Sheets.

## How it's sorted

**Tier 1** — confirmed local address in the listed town. These are your best fits: small
enough that a missed call is real money walking out the door, local enough that "I'm in
your area" outreach lands.

**Tier 2** — shows up serving that town but the real home base is 20–30+ min away, or the
address was ambiguous across listings (noted in the Notes column — verify when you call).
Still worth a call, just lower priority — work through Tier 1 first, town by town.

**Deliberately left off, in every town:** the large regional/multi-decade chains (Carolina
Comfort Air, Weather Master, Air Experts, Crabtree Services, Ideal Services, Allen Kelly &
Co., Biggs HVAC, Bud Matthews, Jackson & Sons, Boer Brothers, Warren-Hay Mechanical). These
read as bigger operations — established for decades, multi-town footprints, likely already
staffed for phones — a worse fit for "we catch the calls your small team can't." Worth
revisiting only if Tier 1/2 dries up everywhere else.

## What to do with it (matches the pitch script)

1. **Call each Tier 1 number once, at lunch (1–3 PM)**, pretending to be a customer with
   a simple question ("hey, do you service my area, roughly what's a service call run?").
   Log the date/time and whether a human answered or it went to voicemail in the CSV's
   last three columns. Work one town at a time so your outreach stays local and coherent.
2. **Voicemail = hot lead.** That's your opener: *"I called Tuesday at 2pm and got your
   voicemail — that's actually why I'm reaching out."* See `../pitch-script.md`.
3. **Human answered = still worth pitching**, just skip the voicemail hook and lead with
   the shadow-mode close from `../outreach-templates.md` instead.
4. Walk in or call the owner directly using the scripts in `../pitch-script.md`.
