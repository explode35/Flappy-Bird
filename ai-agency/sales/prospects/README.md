# Prospect list — Holly Springs, NC HVAC

`holly-springs-hvac.csv` — 14 real HVAC businesses serving Holly Springs, NC, with verified
phone numbers, pulled from BBB/Yelp/company sites. Open it in Sheets/Excel/Numbers, or on
your phone via Google Sheets.

## How it's sorted

**Tier 1 (11 businesses)** — confirmed local address in Holly Springs or immediately
adjacent Fuquay-Varina. These are your best fits: small enough that a missed call is real
money walking out the door, local enough that "I'm in your neighborhood" outreach lands.

**Tier 2 (3 businesses)** — they show up in Holly Springs search results and serve the
area, but their home base is 30+ minutes away (Wendell, Sanford/Pittsboro, Zebulon). Still
worth a call, just lower priority — call Tier 1 first.

**Deliberately left off:** Carolina Comfort Air, Weather Master, Air Experts, Crabtree
Services, Ideal Services. These read as larger regional operations (multi-decade brands,
big service-area footprints, likely already staffed for phones) — worse fit for "we
catch the calls your small team can't." Worth revisiting only if Tier 1/2 dries up.

## What to do with it (matches the pitch script)

1. **Call each Tier 1 number once, at lunch (1–3 PM)**, pretending to be a customer with
   a simple question ("hey, do you service Holly Springs, roughly what's a service call
   run?"). Log the date/time and whether a human answered or it went to voicemail in the
   CSV's last three columns.
2. **Voicemail = hot lead.** That's your opener: *"I called Tuesday at 2pm and got your
   voicemail — that's actually why I'm reaching out."* See `../pitch-script.md`.
3. **Human answered = still worth pitching**, just skip the voicemail hook and lead with
   the shadow-mode close from `../outreach-templates.md` instead.
4. Walk in or call the owner directly using the scripts in `../pitch-script.md`.

## Getting to 50

Holly Springs itself (~pop. 45k) only has so many independent HVAC shops — that's why this
list tops out at 14 solid ones. To build toward the 50-prospect goal in the README's
14-day plan, run this same search-and-filter pass for the neighboring towns your Tier 1
companies already treat as one market: **Fuquay-Varina, Apex, Cary, and Garner.** Same
method: search "[trade] [town] NC", pull the small/independent-looking results, verify
phone numbers, skip the regional chains. Say the word and I'll run the next town.

The same process works for plumbers, roofers, or electricians in any of these towns too,
if you want to diversify beyond HVAC later.
