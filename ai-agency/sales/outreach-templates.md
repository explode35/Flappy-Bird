# Outreach templates

Order of effectiveness for this offer: **walk-in > phone call > SMS > Facebook/Google DM > email.**
Home-service owners live in their trucks and on their phones; they do not read cold email.
Use email only as the follow-up channel after a conversation.

Personalize the [bracketed] parts. Never send more than 2 follow-ups; move on — the niche
is deep and your time is the scarce resource.

## SMS (after finding their number on their website/GMB listing)

**Cold open:**
> Hi [Name] — [You] here, local to [City]. Called [Business] Tuesday around 2pm and got
> voicemail, so this seemed relevant: I run a service that texts your missed callers back
> in 10 seconds and books them before they dial your competitor. 2-min demo runs on your
> phone — want it?

*(Yes, actually call them first and note when voicemail picked up. "I got your voicemail
Tuesday at 2pm" is the whole pitch in one detail — it's proof, not a claim.)*

**Follow-up (day 3):**
> No worries if the timing's off — one number for you before I go: 85% of callers who hit
> voicemail never call back. If you ever want the 2-minute demo, it's here: [link]

## Facebook / Google Business DM

> Hey [Business]! Quick one — I help [trade] companies in [City] catch the calls they miss
> while the crew's on jobs. Missed call → instant text-back → job booked on your calendar,
> automatically. Happy to send a 2-minute demo that runs on your phone. Who's the right
> person to send it to?

## Email (follow-up after a demo or conversation only)

**Subject: the demo + the math**

> [Name] — good talking today. Two things attached:
>
> 1. The demo: [link] (the one where the "customer" books a $4,800 job at 6:47 PM)
> 2. The one-pager with pricing and the 30-day guarantee
>
> Your numbers from our chat: ~[X] missed calls/week × [Y]% close rate × $[Z] average
> ticket ≈ **$[loss]/month** currently going to whoever answers next.
> Triangle Automation is $297/month. That's the whole decision.
>
> I can have it live on your line by [day-after-tomorrow]. Want me to start the paperwork?

## The "shadow mode" close (for fence-sitters)

> Totally get wanting to think it over. Here's a no-cost option: I'll run it in shadow
> mode for a week — it won't text anyone, it just counts the calls you missed and logs
> what they asked for. Next Friday I send you the tally and you decide with your own
> numbers. Fair?

## Referral ask (day 7 after go-live, right after their first "✅ job booked" ping)

> That booking last night was Triangle Automation, by the way 😄 Quick ask: who else do you know
> that's out on jobs all day — buddy with a roofing or plumbing crew? Intro me and I'll
> take $100 off your next month for each one that signs.
