BRIEFING

Rogue data-swarms are crawling the circuit toward your Core. Deploy sentinels on empty board tiles and delete them before they arrive.

DEPLOY Pick a sentinel card at the bottom, then tap any dark tile. Each deploy costs ⚡ energy, earned by destroying intruders and clearing waves.

MERGE Drag a sentinel onto another of the same type and same level to fuse them into the next level — free, and far stronger than two small ones. Max is Lv 5.

MOVE Drag a sentinel onto an empty tile to relocate it at no cost.

UPGRADE Tap a placed sentinel to spend energy on a level, switch its targeting mode, or sell it back for 70% of what it cost.

CALL Call the next wave early for bonus energy — the whole run is timed by pressure, not patience.

CORES Clearing a sector pays ◈ cores. Spend them in the Sentinel Lab on permanent network upgrades and new sentinel classes that carry into every future run.

Leak an intruder and the Core takes damage. Lose all Core integrity and the sector is lost. Finish flawlessly for ★★★.
