## Step 2 — Selected Prospect: Blaine's Barbershop

**Why:** Best review profile of the pool (4.9★ across 151 reviews = an engaged owner and a loyal customer base), a real storefront, steady repeat-service revenue, and a clear conversion story (bookings).

**Digital gap:** Blaine's has no website — customers find them through a Facebook page and a third-party Fresha booking link, while competitor CHOP Barbershop (a franchise in town) runs a polished site with integrated online booking, and dozens of area barbers compete for search traffic on Booksy. Anyone Googling "barber Holly Springs" who lands on a competitor's site never sees Blaine's 4.9-star reputation — the shop's strongest sales asset is invisible at the exact moment people choose a barber.

## Step 3 — Landing Page Concept (built in `blaines-barbershop-landing.html`)

- **Hero:** "A Proper Cut, Right Here in Holly Springs" + Book My Cut / Call CTAs
- **Social proof bar:** "Rated 4.9 stars by 151+ Holly Springs neighbors"
- **Why Us:** Walk out sharp every time · Old-school hot-towel service · Your chair is ready (online booking, 7 a.m. early slots)
- **Services (2-col):** Classic Cut, Skin Fade / Hot Towel Shave, Grey Blending
- **Lead capture:** Name, Phone, Service Needed, Preferred Contact Time → "Reserve My Chair"
- **Visual direction:** Warm ivory + dark walnut + oxblood red + aged brass; barber-pole stripe dividers; engraved serif display type. Vibe: classic American barbershop, trustworthy and established.
