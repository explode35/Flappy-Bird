# Statement of Work — The Corporate Training Architect

**Client:** {client_name}
**Effective date:** {date}
**Package:** {Starter / Signature / Enterprise}

## 1. Scope of services

Provider will produce a structured training curriculum ("the Program")
based on one or more audio recordings provided by Client, consisting of:

- A framework map extracting Client's operational knowledge from the
  recording(s)
- A curriculum outline spanning {N} weeks organized into modules
- A week-by-week instructional pacing guide for each week, following the
  5E model (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate)
- A diagnostic screener for each module, plus a final program diagnostic
- {package-specific add-ons, e.g. slide deck version, LMS export}

## 2. Client responsibilities

- Provide one audio recording of approximately 45 minutes per expert,
  addressing the prompts in the Client Intake Questionnaire
- Respond to any "gaps to clarify" flagged in the framework map within
  5 business days, or Provider will proceed using reasonable judgment and
  note the assumption in the delivered materials
- Designate a single point of contact for feedback during the revision
  period

## 3. Deliverables and timeline

- Draft delivery: within {5} business days of receiving the recording and
  a complete intake questionnaire
- Revision window: {14} days from draft delivery for one round of feedback
  (per package terms)
- Final delivery: within {3} business days of receiving consolidated
  revision feedback

## 4. Fees

- Total fee: ${amount}
- 50% (${deposit}) due upon signing, to book the recording session
- 50% (${balance}) due upon final delivery
- Recording session must occur within 14 days of deposit or the deposit
  is forfeited and rescheduling requires a new deposit

## 5. Intellectual property

Upon full payment, Client owns all rights to the delivered Program
materials. Provider retains the right to use anonymized, non-identifying
excerpts of the Program structure (not Client's specific content) for
Provider's own marketing and portfolio purposes, unless Client opts out in
writing.

## 6. Confidentiality

Provider will treat the audio recording(s), transcript(s), and all
extracted content as confidential and will not share them with any third
party other than the AI service providers necessary to produce the
Program (see Section 7).

## 7. Use of AI processing

Client acknowledges that Provider uses a third-party AI API (Anthropic's
Claude) to process the recording transcript and generate draft content.
Provider reviews and edits all AI-generated material before delivery but
does not warrant that AI-generated content is free of error; Client is
responsible for final review of factual and compliance-sensitive content
before use in training delivery.

## 8. Limitation of liability

Provider's total liability under this agreement is limited to the total
fees paid by Client. Provider is not liable for indirect, incidental, or
consequential damages arising from use of the Program.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate with written notice before the recording
session is delivered, in which case the deposit is refunded less any
work-in-progress costs already incurred. Once a draft has been delivered,
fees are non-refundable but Client retains all rights to materials
delivered to date.
