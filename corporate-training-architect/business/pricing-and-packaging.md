# Pricing & Packaging

## Positioning

Anchor against the alternative the client is actually comparing you to: an
instructional designer or L&D consultant charging by the week, or the
opportunity cost of a senior person's time spent writing training materials
instead of billing/producing. You are cheaper and faster than both, and the
gap should feel obvious, not close.

## Packages

### Starter — $2,500
- Up to 8-week program
- Framework map + curriculum outline + weekly 5E guides
- One module-level diagnostic screener (not per-module)
- One revision round (facilitator feedback on up to 3 weeks)
- Delivery: 3 business days after recording received

### Signature — $7,500 (anchor offer, lead with this)
- Full 18-week program
- Framework map + curriculum outline + all 18 weekly 5E guides
- Diagnostic screener per module + final program diagnostic
- One revision round (facilitator feedback on any weeks/screeners)
- 30-minute walkthrough call to hand off the package
- Delivery: 5 business days after recording received

### Enterprise — $15,000+
- Everything in Signature
- Up to 3 expert recordings merged into one unified program (e.g. three
  senior reps whose combined expertise becomes one sales-onboarding track)
- Custom program length (26+ weeks) or multi-track programs (e.g. separate
  tracks for IC vs. manager onboarding)
- Two revision rounds
- Live facilitator training session (you teach their trainers how to run
  week 1 as a model)
- Priority delivery: 3 business days

## Cost basis (for your own margin math)

API cost per full 18-week run is typically **$8-$20** in Claude Opus 4.8
tokens (see `manifest.json` after a run for actuals — it logs
`usage_totals` you can multiply by current per-token pricing). Your real
cost is the 2-4 hours of human review and light editing per engagement.
At $7,500 for ~3 hours of hands-on work plus trivial API cost, this is a
high-margin service — price for the value delivered (a finished training
program), not for the hours worked.

## Payment terms

- 50% deposit to book the recording session, 50% on delivery.
- Recording session must happen within 14 days of deposit or it's
  forfeited (protects your calendar from indefinite holds).
- Revisions must be requested within 14 days of delivery.
